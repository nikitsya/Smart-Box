## Ongoing individual requirements

- Weekly attendance, stand-ups and Universal Design tutorial Q&A.
- Sprint review and show-and-tell every two weeks, or as required.
- Progress review interviews where required.
- Evidence of individual roles and contributions through Scrumwise, GitHub and lab work.
- A reflective report for every student; an individual interview where required.

**Assessment weighting:** group CA **70%** (non-recoverable); individual CA **30%**.
